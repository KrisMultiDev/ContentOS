ContentOS · Visual direction

Fieldhouse Green

Pine green as the working accent on warm, green-biased neutrals — a production binder, not a SaaS gradient. Segoe UI (native on Windows) carries the interface; Cascadia Mono carries the reel codes that stitch the whole system together. Status colors stay separate from the brand green so “good news” and “our brand” never blur.
